# OomniEye Digital Twin Approval & Maintenance Policy (Rev 2.4)

## Section 1: Drone Patrol Video Approvals (Surveillance & Airspace)

All Level 2 autonomous drone video submissions must satisfy three non-negotiable verification criteria prior to operator sign-off:

1. Perimeter Sweep Verification: The flight video must show continuous boundary tracking at minimum 1080p / 60fps resolution without frame stuttering.
2. Geofence & Altitude Telemetry: Flight log telemetry overlay must demonstrate altitude adherence between 15m and 45m AGL with zero unauthorized incursions into civilian exclusion corridors.
3. Timestamp & Pilot Licensing: Alex HelpAdmin or certified remote pilots must supply a synchronized UTC timestamp matching the physical command centre telemetry clock.

## Section 2: Sensor & Equipment Technical Specifications (Hardware & Safety)

Hardware specification documents submitted by Sam HelpAdmin require rigorous electrical and environmental sign-off:

1. Calibration & Compliance: IoT vibration, pressure, and thermal sensor documentation must display active ISO 9001 and OSHA Class 1 Div 2 certifications.
2. Recalibration Cycle Threshold: Equipment whose factory calibration expiration date is within 30 days must be flagged and rejected back to the technician.
3. Fail-Safe Battery Backups: All hazard-zone sensors must provide schematics verifying at least 72 hours of uninterrupted secondary battery runtime upon primary grid disconnection.

## Section 3: Spatial Zone Layouts & Camera Overlap (Digital Twin Mapping)

Spatial maps submitted by Elena HelpAdmin establish the real-time coordinates for digital-twin overlays:

1. Camera Overlap Integrity: Adjacent surveillance cameras must maintain an optical field-of-view overlap of at least 15% to eliminate surveillance blind spots.
2. Coordinate Reference System: All floor plans and camera map images must adhere to the WGS84 datum with centimeter-accurate local origin offsets.
3. Review Turnaround SLA: Review must occur within 24 hours of submission to prevent camera telemetry mismatches during live monitoring shifts.

## Section 4: Escalation Protocols & Standard Operator Actions

1. High Priority Items: Videos and Sensor PDFs are considered mission-critical. If unreviewed after 48 hours, they auto-escalate to the Shift Supervisor.
2. Sign-off Procedures: Operators must inspect attached telemetry, verify checklist items sequentially, and record approval notes before changing status to "Approved".
